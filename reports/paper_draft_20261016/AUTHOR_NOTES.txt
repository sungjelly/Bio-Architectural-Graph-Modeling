SCOPE
The draft follows the currently training original Relative-Geometric QKV model selected by the author. Geometry contributes an additive attention bias. It does not describe the separate geometry-modulated cosine-attention variant.
The earlier SO2 visual observations and layer analyses are clearly distinguished from the current donor-separated training/validation study. Qualitative PCA/scVI comparison reflects the author's initial account; a matched archived scVI benchmark was not found. No numerical accuracy or pathology validation was invented.
