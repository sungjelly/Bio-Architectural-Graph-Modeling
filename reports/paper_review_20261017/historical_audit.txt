Historical claims audit, 2026-10-17. Read-only audit of aggregate reports; no experiments or raw/clinical rows accessed.

OVERALL
The supplied paper combines historical 14-core SO2 standardized-log1p results with the newer 28-core Pearson-residual model. Those scopes must be separated. Representation partition changes are verified; independent cancer-region agreement or superiority over PCA is not verified by the reviewed records.

1. CLINICIAN-IDENTIFIED CANCER REGIONS / PCA
Manuscript abstract/results state that BAGM showed clearer correspondence with clinician-identified cancer regions than PCA and that h4 improved delineation. The current h0/hL campaign is explicitly exploratory, fully observed and transductive; it supports descriptive partition differences, not cancer-region accuracy or biological integration. It states no clinical-label analysis was planned. The current reserved-test clustering campaign states manual cancer ROI inputs have not yet been located.
Source: experiments/campaigns/cmp_20261017_so12_h0_hl_leiden_segmentation/README.md:21-38; experiments/campaigns/cmp_20261017_so12_paper_test_leiden/README.md:18-26,47-55.
Recommended replacement: "Exploratory Leiden maps showed different spatial partitions before and after graph processing. Preliminary author-reported visual impressions suggested correspondence with suspected cancer regions, but independent region annotations and quantitative comparisons are required to establish cancer-region delineation or superiority over PCA."
Stricter alternative, if no author confirmation is available: omit the visual-superiority assertion and state only the verified partition differences. Do not present the author's observation as an independently audited result.

Current completed analysis uses the best epoch-151 checkpoint r_20261016T090820Z_ad267085_s000_f00_a02_451ef28b, all observed genes, all 407,659 mapped cells across 28 cores. h0 is trained node-encoder output; hL/h4 is fourth block output before decoding. Seed-0 h0/hL cluster counts are 16/22; h0-to-hL ARI is 0.370156. Leiden seed0/seed1 ARI is 0.806953 for h0 and 0.883709 for hL. These are algorithmic partition agreement measures, not cancer accuracy, training-seed stability or independent-donor replication.
Source: experiments/campaigns/cmp_20261017_so12_h0_hl_leiden_segmentation/README.md:10-16,42-58,218-230.
h0/hL uses all256 dimensions, cosine exact kNN30, unweighted union graph, Leiden to convergence. Prior PCA-family methods use32 dimensions, Euclidean HNSW, weighted graphs, two Leiden iterations. Therefore encoder superiority is confounded with analysis-pipeline differences.

5. BCE/HURDLE ALREADY STUDIED
Statement that binary BCE/hurdle comparisons are merely future work ignores completed historical studies, although a controlled same-architecture/same-split loss ablation remains needed. Sep30 binary BCE endpoint: precision68.6825%,recall7.9058%,AP0.298425 (4096-bin descending-threshold average precision, not trapezoidal PR area); observed prevalence0.081178. Hurdle comparison has precision68.8231%,recall8.0431%, but no saved hurdle AUPRC. Different selected objectives remain an asymmetry. This is a different target-isolated architecture/task, allowing fully observed neighbor expression, single-seed validation selection on two cores/one donor.
Source: reports/analyses/so2_binary_expression_bce/r_20260930T091905Z_4be167c3_s000_f00_a01_ffc3b863/v1/report.md:5-7,16-25,27-53,61-63.
Recommended discussion: "Separate occurrence and hurdle-model studies have been explored, but a matched comparison holding architecture, preprocessing, masking and split fixed is still needed to attribute performance differences to the loss."
The current sentence that Huber itself has not been shown to cause poor performance is appropriate. Sep6 constant-predictor analysis found Huber optimum below MSE gene mean for999/1000 genes but explicitly did not identify a neural causal effect.
