BAGM manuscript consistency audit — 17 October 2026
Read-only audit of source/campaign records; no raw patient data accessed.

DECISION
The supplied paper is not fully consistent with current work. It mixes the current
joint-slide Pearson experiment with the historical geometry-modulated cosine
model and unsupported pathology-comparison language. A revised draft should
distinguish completed exploratory evidence from the active paper-specific run.

1. ARCHITECTURE: MATERIAL ERROR
Appendix A and Figure 1 describe geometry-conditioned feature gates, L2-normalized
Q/K, a bounded trainable temperature, and tanh biases. These belong to the
September SO2 geometry-modulated variant, not the current original Relative-QKV.
Current model: four independent pre-LayerNorm blocks; width 256, eight heads of
32 dimensions. Per head, for sender j and receiver i:
 q_i = W_Q LN(h_i), k_j = W_K LN(h_j), v_j = W_V LN(h_j)
 s_ij = (q_i dot k_j)/sqrt(32) + b_ij
 b_ij = W_2 GELU(LN(W_1 rho_ij + a_1)) + a_2
 alpha_ij = softmax over incoming neighbors j of s_ij
 z_i = sum_j alpha_ij v_j.
Geometry network: 70 -> 128 -> LN -> GELU -> 8; its final weights AND bias
are zero-initialized. It has no feature gate, no Q/K normalization, no learned
temperature, and no tanh bounding. Geometry directly enters logits only.
Output projection is bias-free; residual/FFN and decoder dimensions in the
paper otherwise agree. Values are projections of encoded cell states containing
visible RNA, mask and morphology information plus prior graph context; they
are not exclusively molecular/RNA content. No implicit or explicit self edges.
Self information is retained through residual paths.

2. METHODS: NECESSARY SPECIFICATION
407,659 mapped cells, 28 cores, two slides, 1,000 biological RNA genes and
22 morphology/imaging covariates. RNA measurements and selected image-derived
intensities are inputs; this is not a jointly modeled protein-expression panel.
340 unmapped SO2 FOV246 cells are excluded (existing cohort definition).
Graph: coordinate-only within-core radial-stratified selection with nominal
200 neighbors before bidirectional union, maximum 500 micrometres; final degree
can exceed 200. Shell quotas 48/64/48/40 in 0-50/50-150/150-300/300-500 um.
No self or cross-core/slide edges; no absolute coordinates or IDs as node inputs.
70 geometry features: 64 distance RBFs plus receiver/source axial alignment,
orientation-tensor alignment, receiver/source anisotropy and normalized distance.
These encode spatial context and do not establish direct contact/communication.
Evidence: current config:60-150; src/spatial_benchmark/relative_geometry.py:1-46.

Each graph cell independently draws a masked-gene count uniformly from integers
0 through 1,000 inclusive, with positions sampled without replacement. Ten
training views/core/epoch, three fixed evaluation views/core. This is a broad
partial-gene masking law with rare zero/whole-cell endpoints, not a dedicated
whole-node prediction benchmark. Morphology remains visible.
Train-fitted quasi-Poisson Pearson transform (phi 1.01), residual mean/sample SD,
upper clip 10. Input exposure is estimated from visible counts divided by visible
training gene-frequency mass, clamped at one; fully masked expression is zero.
Fully observed transformed arrays are supervised targets ONLY. Hidden full-cell
totals cannot enter inputs. The target is standardized residual features, not
raw transcript counts or a count likelihood. Normalization supports joint-slide
analysis but does not establish removal of batch effects.

src/spatial_benchmark/so12_relative_qkv_data.py:122-171.

5. COMPLETED REPRESENTATION EVIDENCE AND CANCER LANGUAGE
The previous model's fully observed h0/h4 representations were clustered jointly
across all 407,659 cells, including train and validation. h0/h4 yielded 16/22
primary clusters, between-representation ARI 0.3701562886. Two Leiden seeds on
each fixed graph gave ARI 0.8069532158 / 0.8837090008. These measure algorithmic
stability, not independent model seeds or biological reproducibility.
PCA-family comparison uses different representation dimensions, distances,
neighbor methods, edge weighting and Leiden convergence settings, so it does
not isolate encoder quality. h4-h0 also includes FFN/residual transformations.
Clinician cancer-region masks have not been located; no verified quantitative
pathology scoring exists in the audited current campaigns. Paper statements
that BAGM showed clearer correspondence with clinician-identified cancer than
PCA should become explicitly author-reported preliminary visual impressions,
if retained at all. No validated cancer boundary superiority is supported.

6. PRECISION / RECALL / AUPRC
The current Pearson regression run reports masked Huber, MAE, MSE and Pearson,
not a matched binary occurrence benchmark. The manuscript does not specify
which historical endpoint, target threshold, prediction scale, masks, population
or PR integration definition produced these statements. Do not assign historical
count-detection limitations to current Pearson model performance. Either omit
the metric claim, or explicitly label it historical, name its endpoint and
evaluation definition and supply verified values. AP and trapezoidal PR AUC
must not be interchanged. Larger datasets alone will not resolve this ambiguity.
Historical nonzero comparison: cmp_20260905_so2_nonzero_accuracy/README.md:
25-57,144-189 (fitted-cohort standardized-log1p models; fixed count threshold .5,
recall approximately 13%, not current held-out Pearson evidence).

7. INTERPRETATION
Attention is computational routing, not biological importance. Jacobians are
local model sensitivities; specify raw-count versus normalized-feature scale,
hold mask/graph fixed, and distinguish one-hop from multihop paths. A raw-count
derivative includes the normalization's cross-gene exposure pathway. No current
matched self-only, geometry-removal, graph-null or independent pathology test
establishes graph-specific benefit, mechanism or causal influence.

Recommended manuscript scope: exploratory BAGM methodology plus accurately
labeled completed predecessor evidence, with the current paper-core experiment
described as ongoing. Retain a dated status and a visible change log.
